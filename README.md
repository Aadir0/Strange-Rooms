# Starnge-Rooms 
### This game was made for the Brackeys GameJam.
A group of boys are playing cricket beside an abandoned, mysterious house when a powerful hit sends the ball through a broken window. The older brother enters the house to retrieve it but never comes back out. As hours pass, worry drives his younger brother to enter the house in search of him. Inside, the building reveals itself as a living labyrinth made of strange, ever-changing rooms where reality feels wrong — controls may change, doors behave unpredictably, and hidden traps react to the player’s actions. Each room contains puzzles, interactive objects, and two locked doors that must both be opened before the player can choose a path deeper into the house. The boy slowly uncovers clues that the house is controlled by a witch who traps visitors by confusing and testing them. As he progresses, he learns to manipulate environmental traps and strange devices to survive. Eventually he reaches a chamber where his brother is imprisoned in a cage, and the witch appears. Instead of direct combat, the younger brother uses the knowledge and mechanics learned from previous rooms — activating portals, triggering traps, and manipulating environmental hazards — to defeat her indirectly. With the witch’s control broken, the cage unlocks, the brothers escape together, and the house collapses back into silence, leaving behind unanswered mysteries.

### Movement & Control Mechanics
- W or Up arrow - Move Up
- S or Down arrow - Move Down
- A or Left arrow - Move Left
- D or Right arrow - Move Right
- E - Interact with Objects

### Strange Room Rules (Non-Physics-Based)
- Control variations (e.g., reversed inputs or rotated movement).
- Hidden paths revealed by interaction or observation.
- Light or visual cues revealing invisible objects.
- Doors with reversed logic (activating switch closes instead of opens).
